Chicken biryani is a flavorful and aromatic dish that originated in South Asia.
It is very interesting matter to know how chicken biriyani is prepared. The preparation of chicken biriyani involves various stages .
It can be prepared at home easily. At first ,water is boiled in a saucepan. Then washed rice is added .Next ,it is boiled for 5 minutes.
The water is then drained, and the rice is set aside. In a separate pan, the chicken is cooked with a blend of aromatic spices and curd.
After that ,the biryani is layered, starting with rice, followed by the chicken mixture, and this process is repeated twice.
The layers are garnished with cumin seeds and ginger for an extra burst of flavor. The biryani is allowed to simmer for an additional 10 minutes,( ensuring that the rice absorbs the rich essence of the seasoned chicken).
This step-by-step process results in a tantalizing chicken biriyani, showcasing the perfect amalgamation of spices and textures.

Ingredients

  • 50g Chicken
  • 100g Rice
  • 10g Spices
  • 50g Curd
  1. Boil the chicken for 15mins in a stove
  2. Add Spices after 15 mins and mash it properly.
  3. Add curd to this mixture
  4. Add the resulting mixture into the pot of rice
